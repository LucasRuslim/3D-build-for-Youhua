# Handoff: classroom props and school weapons (for an AI assistant)

You are receiving ready-made **Godot 4.3+** assets for a multiplayer brawl game:
a green plastic school desk and chair that can be pushed, picked up and thrown,
plus eight stationery weapons that can also stab or swing, a bow with arrows,
and ten everyday school items (including a foam-spraying fire extinguisher).
All of them stay in sync over LAN or online play. Everything below is already built
and tested. Your job is usually just to wire it into the game's player and level.

## Rules that matter

- Put the folders at **`res://assets/classroom_furniture/`**,
  **`res://assets/stationery/`**, **`res://assets/archery/`** and
  **`res://assets/school_items/`** exactly. The scenes reference files by those
  paths. The scripts build on each other (`Bow`, `Arrow`, `FireExtinguisher`
  and every school item use `StationeryWeapon`, which extends `NetworkedProp`),
  so everything needs the furniture and stationery folders. If it has to live elsewhere, move it inside the
  Godot editor (FileSystem panel) so the references are rewritten, or
  search-and-replace the `res://assets/...` prefixes in the `.tscn` files.
- Engine: **Godot 4.3 or newer** (tested on 4.3 and 4.5), GDScript. It doesn't
  work in Godot 3. It is fine in a C# Godot 4 project.
- Units are metres, +Y up. Furniture: the origin is on the floor under each
  object and the student-facing side is +Z. Stationery: the origin is the grip
  and the tip, blade or front points along −Z, the direction the hands aim.
- Don't edit the `.glb` files by hand. They are generated by
  `tools/generate_assets.py` (furniture; then `tools/make_scenes.py` if
  colliders change) and `tools/generate_stationery.py` (stationery models,
  textures and scenes), `tools/generate_archery.py` (bow and arrow) and
  `tools/generate_school_items.py` (school items). They need numpy + Pillow. Change a script and run it
  again.

## Networking model (server-authoritative)

- **Who simulates:** the prop's multiplayer authority (default peer 1, the host)
  runs real physics. On every other peer the body is `freeze = true` with
  `FREEZE_MODE_KINEMATIC`, so it is still solid, and it smoothly follows the
  replicated `net_position`, `net_rotation`, `net_linear_velocity` and
  `holder_peer_id`. Replication is *on-change* at about 30 Hz, so props at rest
  send nothing.
- **How actions reach the host:** clients never move props directly. They
  call methods that send RPCs to the authority, which validates them: in range,
  not already held, and one prop per player.
- **Offline / local play:** with no multiplayer peer, Godot uses
  `OfflineMultiplayerPeer` (id 1), so the same code works.
- **Props placed in a level:** they sync automatically if every peer loads the
  same level, because node paths must match.
- **Props spawned at runtime:** add both `.tscn` files to a
  `MultiplayerSpawner`'s auto-spawn list.

## API

### `NetworkedProp` (the desk/chair root)

**Signals.** These are emitted on the authority/host only.
- `picked_up(peer_id)`, `dropped(peer_id)`, `thrown(peer_id, velocity)`.
- `hit(body, impact_speed, thrower_peer_id)`: `thrower_peer_id` is 0 if nobody
  threw it in the last 3 s. If the hit body has
  `on_prop_hit(prop, impact_speed, thrower_peer_id)`, that is called too. Use it
  for damage, and replicate health yourself.

**Methods.** Call these on any peer.
- `request_pickup()`, `request_throw()`, `request_drop()`, `is_held()`.
- `push(push_velocity: Vector3, at_global: Vector3)`: a client forwards this to
  the host, and the speed is clamped by `max_push_speed`.

**Static helpers.**
- `NetworkedProp.push_from_character(character: CharacterBody3D, velocity_before_move: Vector3)`.
  A `CharacterBody3D` can't push rigid bodies by itself, so call this right after
  `move_and_slide()`:

  ```gdscript
  var v := velocity
  move_and_slide()
  NetworkedProp.push_from_character(self, v)
  ```

- `NetworkedProp.find_holder(tree, peer_id)` and
  `NetworkedProp.find_prop_held_by(tree, peer_id)`.

**Inspector tuning (exports).**
- Throwing: `throw_speed` (13), `throw_lift` (0.18), `throw_spin` (6).
- Holding: `max_pickup_distance` (2.5), `hold_offset` (Transform3D, where the
  prop sits relative to the hands).
- Damage: `damage_min_speed` (4), `thrower_credit_time` (3).
- Pushing: `push_strength` (1), `max_push_speed` (12).
- Network smoothing: `interpolation_speed` (18), `snap_distance` (3).

### `PropHolder` (child `Node3D` of the player, placed at the hands)

**Requirements.**
- **Group:** it adds itself to group `prop_holders`, which is how the host finds
  the hands of a peer.
- **Ownership:** its multiplayer authority must be the controlling peer. Calling
  `set_multiplayer_authority(peer_id)` on the player root, which is recursive,
  does that.
- **Replication:** the player's transform must be replicated to the host.

**Exports.**
- `aim_node`: optional, for example the camera. Its −Z is the throw direction.
- `reach`: default 1.8 m.

**Grab choice:** `find_grab_target()` picks the free prop within `reach`
(measured to its collision surface) with the lowest *surface distance + centre
distance*. That means a pencil lying on a desk beats the desk, and the chair
you're touching beats a pencil on the next desk.

**Methods.**
- `toggle_grab_throw()`: the first call grabs, the second throws.
- `try_grab()`, `throw()`, `drop()`, `held_prop()`, `find_grab_target()`.
- `get_hold_transform()`, `get_aim_direction()`, `get_holder_body()`.

### `StationeryWeapon` (each stationery root; everything from `NetworkedProp` too)

**Signal** (on the host): `weapon_hit(body, damage, attacker_peer_id, kind)`,
where `kind` is `"stab"`, `"swing"` or `"throw"`.

**Damage delivery:** the host calls
`on_weapon_hit(weapon, damage, attacker_peer_id, kind)` on the hit body if it
has that method, otherwise `on_prop_hit(prop, impact_speed, thrower)`. Never
both, so there is no double damage.

**Methods:** `request_attack()` (any peer; the host checks the holder and the
cooldown), `is_stuck()`, `tip_direction()`. `PropHolder.attack()` calls
`request_attack()` on the held item.

**Behaviour:**
- **Stab** (`attack_style = STAB`): lunge animation, and the first body along
  the aim line within `melee_range` takes `melee_damage`.
- **Swing** (`SWING`): arc animation, and every body in a wide sphere in front
  takes `melee_damage`. Loose rigid bodies get knocked aside.
- Static bodies never take melee hits, and the holder's own body is excluded.
- **Throw:** damage = impact speed × `throw_damage_per_speed`. `pointy` items
  steer tip-first in flight, deal ×`tip_first_bonus` when they land tip first,
  and stick into `StaticBody3D`s at ≥ `stick_min_speed`. They stay frozen there
  until someone grabs them.
- Continuous collision detection is switched on only while an item flies
  faster than 4 m/s. Colliders are at least 16 mm thick. Thinner or always-on
  CCD made these light items sink into desks in Godot Physics.

**Default tuning:**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pencil | stab | 12 | 0.8 | 0.4 | 18 | 1.0 | yes | 0.05 |
| ballpoint_pen | stab | 10 | 0.8 | 0.4 | 18 | 0.9 | yes | 0.05 |
| ruler | swing | 8 | 1.0 | 0.5 | 14 | 0.7 | no | 0.08 |
| scissors | stab | 18 | 0.85 | 0.55 | 15 | 1.2 | yes | 0.15 |
| compass | stab | 15 | 0.85 | 0.5 | 16 | 1.1 | yes | 0.12 |
| stapler | swing | 16 | 0.9 | 0.7 | 12 | 1.4 | no | 0.6 |
| eraser | swing | 4 | 0.8 | 0.35 | 16 | 0.6 | no | 0.05 |
| pencil_case | swing | 7 | 1.0 | 0.6 | 12 | 0.8 | no | 0.35 |

### `Bow` and `Arrow` (`assets/archery/scripts/`)

**Model conventions.**
- **Bow:** origin at the grip, held upright (limbs along ±Y), shoots along −Z,
  string on the +Z side. `hold_offset` puts it 25 cm in front of the hands. The
  string is two thin `BoxMesh` children that `bow.gd` updates each frame from
  `draw_amount`.
- **Arrow:** origin at mid-shaft, tip −Z, nock at +Z `nock_offset` (0.345).

**How they work together (all host-side, requested through `PropHolder`):**
- **Loading:** when the hands hold a bow, `PropHolder.try_grab()` and
  `toggle_grab_throw()` call `bow.can_collect(target)` and
  `bow.request_collect(target)` instead of grabbing. The arrow becomes
  `bow_state = NOCKED` (first) or `STOWED` (hidden). Loaded arrows report
  `is_held() == true`, have collision disabled, and follow
  `bow.nock_transform_for(arrow)`.
- **Shooting:** `PropHolder.attack()` maps to `bow.request_attack()`, which
  starts the draw when loaded and swings as a club when empty.
  `PropHolder.attack_release()` maps to `bow.request_release()`, which shoots:
  speed = `shoot_speed × lerp(min_draw, 1, charge)`, where charge = draw time ÷
  `draw_time`.
- **After the shot:** the arrow is launched along the holder's aim, ignoring the
  archer and the bow briefly, and the next arrow is nocked. The bow emits
  `shot(arrow, shooter_peer_id, speed)`.
- **Arrow hits:** fired arrows report hits with kind `"shot"`, credited to the
  shooter. They stick into static bodies and can be collected again from there.
- **Spilling:** `dropped`/`thrown` on the bow (including the holder
  disconnecting) spills all carried arrows as loose props.

**Defaults:**
- Bow: `max_arrows` 12, `shoot_speed` 40, `min_draw` 0.3, `draw_time` 0.7 s,
  `draw_length` 0.25 m, `collect_range` 2.0. Melee swing 9.
- Arrow: `throw_damage_per_speed` 0.8 with a 1.5× tip-first bonus, so a full
  draw does about 48 damage and a quick tap about 15. Hand stab 8.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fire_extinguisher | swing | 20 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 6 | 9 | 2.0 | 4.0 | spray + burst, see below |
| broom | swing | 9 | 1.7 | 0.7 | 3 | 12 | 0.6 | 0.6 | |
| umbrella | stab | 11 | 1.35 | 0.55 | 2 | 14 | 0.8 | 0.45 | |

| lunch_tray | swing | 10 | 1.0 | 0.55 | 3 | 14 | 0.9 | 0.4 | `flat_spin`, 4 cm collider |

**New `StationeryWeapon` exports used here:**
- `melee_knockback` (m/s): calls `apply_knockback(velocity)` on the hit body
  if it has that method.
- `flat_spin`: thrown items spin around their own up axis.
- `ccd_in_flight`: default true. CCD is used only while flying faster than
  4 m/s; it kills bounces, so the ball turns it off.

**`FireExtinguisher`:**
- **Model:** origin at the carry handle, body hanging below, nozzle along −Z
  at `nozzle` (0, −0.05, −0.175).
- **Spraying:** `request_attack()` starts spraying when `spray_charge > 0`
  (otherwise it swings); `request_release()` stops. While spraying, the host
  runs a check every 0.1 s, at most 256 bodies (a busy room has many in
  range). Bodies whose collision-shape centre is within `spray_range` (4 m)
  and `spray_angle_deg` (22°) of the aim, with a clear ray, get
  `spray_damage_per_second × 0.1` damage (kind `"spray"`),
  `apply_knockback(aim × spray_push_speed)` (6 m/s), and loose props an
  impulse of `spray_prop_push × 0.1 × min(mass, 10)`.
- **Charge:** `spray_charge` drains over `spray_duration` (8 s); `refill()` is
  host-side.
- **Burst:** a thrown impact ≥ `burst_min_speed` (9 m/s) with charge > 0.2
  uses 0.35 charge. It deals `burst_damage` (10, kind `"burst"`) plus
  knockback within `burst_radius` (2.5 m), emits `burst(position)`, and plays
  a particle puff on every peer.
- **Visuals:** foam is a `CPUParticles3D` child, emitting while the synced
  `spraying` is true.

### Minimal player wiring

```gdscript
# player.gd (CharacterBody3D), with a PropHolder child named "Hands"
func _enter_tree():
    set_multiplayer_authority(name.to_int())   # if players are named by peer id

func _physics_process(delta):
    if not is_multiplayer_authority(): return
    # ... set velocity from input ...
    var v := velocity
    move_and_slide()
    NetworkedProp.push_from_character(self, v)

func _unhandled_input(event):
    if not is_multiplayer_authority(): return
    if event.is_action_pressed("grab_throw"): $Hands.toggle_grab_throw()
    if event.is_action_pressed("drop"): $Hands.drop()
    if event.is_action_pressed("attack"): $Hands.attack()   # stab / swing / draw bow
    if event.is_action_released("attack"): $Hands.attack_release()   # shoot bow

func on_weapon_hit(weapon, damage, attacker_id, kind):   # runs on the host
    pass  # apply `damage` here (stationery)

func on_prop_hit(prop, speed, thrower_id):   # runs on the host
    pass  # apply damage here (furniture: scale by impact speed)

func apply_knockback(v: Vector3):   # runs on the host
    velocity += v
```

## Verified behaviour (Godot 4.3, headless tests in `tests/`)

**Physics and offline play:**
- Props settle upright, and the colliders match the meshes.
- They can be grabbed and follow the hands, and throws leave at about 13 m/s.
- Wall hits are reported, credited to the thrower.
- A `CharacterBody3D` running at 5 m/s shoves the desk or chair about 5 m and
  knocks it over. Without `push_from_character` it moves 0 m.

**Two-process ENet over localhost:**
- The host spawns the client's hands, and the client sees host-simulated props
  as kinematic.
- The client grabs and throws a chair. Both sides see it fly, and the host
  credits hits to the client.
- The client's character shoves a chair through the host.
- The client grabs a pencil off a desk and stabs a target on the host (12
  damage, credited to the client). It throws the pencil into a wall, and both
  sides see it stuck there.

- The client picks up the bow, loads an arrow, and both sides see the string
  draw. The shot sticks in a wall, and the host credits it to the client as a
  `"shot"` (about 48 damage).

- The client picks up a fire extinguisher and sprays a target on the host.
  Both sides see the spray, and the host registers repeated foam hits plus
  knockback for the client.

**School items (offline, every item):** rests on the floor, gets picked up,
hits (and knocks back) for its listed damage, and does damage when thrown.
Trays and books spin flat, and the basketball bounces back to about 64% of its
drop height. The extinguisher's spray hits and pushes a target in its cone,
misses one outside the cone and one behind a wall, shoves a chair, and uses up
foam. When empty it swings, and thrown into a wall it bursts and knocks back a
bystander.

**Grab choice:** hands over a desk pick the pencil on it, not the desk; hands
beside a chair pick the chair, not the compass on the desk.

**Archery (offline):** three arrows load (one nocked, two stowed and hidden,
none grabbable by others). A full draw shoots at 40 m/s and sticks tip-first
12 m away. A quick tap shoots at about 12 m/s and hits a character. A stuck
arrow is collected back, dropping the bow spills its arrows, the empty bow
swings, and an arrow stabs by hand.

**Stationery (offline, every item):**
- Rests on the floor and on desks without sinking (0 of 96 drops at different
  angles).
- One melee hit per cooldown for exactly `melee_damage`, with the right kind.
- Thrown damage on walls and characters.
- Pointy items stick into the wall and can be grabbed back out; blunt ones
  bounce off.

**Hit detection:** fast props sweep one physics step ahead, because continuous
collision detection can stop a body before Godot reports any contact.
